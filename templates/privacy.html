Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects only the information necessary to provide the Service: your email address and password for account creation, and the expense entries you voluntarily add, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect any sensitive financial information, such as bank account or card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data you enter is used solely to operate the Service: displaying your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and filtering by time period. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers and is accessible only to your account. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party service providers, such as hosting or font delivery services, to operate the Service. These providers may process limited technical data (such as IP address or browser type) as necessary to deliver their services. We do not permit them to use your data for any other purpose.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or if you would like to access, correct, or delete your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example. You may also delete your account and associated data at any time from the profile page.
